Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Citation Assistant provides citation recommendations based on text selected by the user within Google Docs.

Information Processed

The application processes only the text explicitly selected by the user for citation recommendation purposes.

Use of Information

Selected text is transmitted to the Citation Assistant backend service to generate citation recommendations.

The information is used solely to provide the citation recommendation functionality requested by the user.

Data Sharing

Citation Assistant does not sell personal information and does not share user data with third parties except as necessary to provide the service.
